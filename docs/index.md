# San Diego Recommendations

We lived in San Diego, CA for 11yrs. This is a list of places to eat, see, etc. Caveat: we moved away from San Diego in 2021, but return somewhat regularly (every other year or so). Things may have changed.

## Food

* Mexican  
    * Carmen’s Mexican Food (Clairemont Mesa). This is one of our regular taco shops. In the winter the tortilla soup is excellent.  
    * Harvest Mexican (Kearny Mesa). This is one of our regular taco shops. Shrimp burrito is good.  
    * Caliente (Sorrento Valley). One of Lee’s prior regular taco shops because it was close to offices I used to work at. Adobada or carnitas burritos.  
    * Vallarta (drive thru’s in Clairemont Mesa and Mira Mesa). Good if you need a “quick” drive through burrito. The salsa is habanero based and has a good kick.  
    * Don Carlos (La Jolla). If you’re in La Jolla and need a burrito this is a good stop. This is our regular source for California burritos. Carne asada is good too, as are the tamales.  
* Pizza  
    * Regents Pizza (University City). \~30 taps of local craft beers. Lee used to go here after work. Had worked up to level 3 of 4 on the hot wing heat.  
    * Bronx Pizza. The pizza only comes in one size \- large. The white pizza is amazing.  
    * Blind Lady Ale House (Normal Heights)  
    * Pizza Port (Solana Beach and Ocean Beach). Solana Beach is the original one, parking is difficult though. Beer buddies, Swami’s IPA, and a pie.  
* Pubs and Breweries  
    * O’Brien’s (Convoy / Kearny Mesa). Irish pub with a large and rotating list of local craft beers. Occasionally they get Pliny the Younger on tap. This is a limited batch IIIPA from Russian River Brewing. Garlic fries and a burger. Skip the parking lot and find street parking or the paid lot around the corner.  
    * Societe (Kearny Mesa)  
    * Alesmith (Miramar)  
    * Ballast Point (Little Italy, Miramar)  
    * Green Flash (Sorrento Valley)  
    * Viewpoint (Del Mar)  
    * Duck Foot  
    * Second Chance  
    * New English  
    * Modern times (North Park)  
    * White Labs (Miramar). Go here to taste the same beer made with different yeast  
* Asian  
    * Dumpling Inn (Convoy / Kearny Mesa). The best dumplings / dim sum in town.  
    * Sushi Deli 1, 3\. (Downtown, Kearny Mesa, respectively). One of our regular sushi places.  
    * Poke 1-½. This is a chain (our regular location was in Kearny Mesa), but the poke is excellent for the price.  
* Other  
    * Brazil by the Bay (Sports Arena). Hole in the wall place for Brazillian food. Can’t go wrong with feijoada and a guarana with brigadeiro for desert.  
    * El Pescador (La Jolla). Fishmonger with fresh made sandwiches via counter service.  
    * Caroline’s Seaside Cafe (UCSD/Scripps). Good place for breakfast or lunch. Patio is right above the beach by the Scripps Pier. Parking in the nearby lot is only free on weekends, otherwise, you need to find spots on the street.  
    * Mochinut (Kearny Mesa). Mochi based donuts.  
    * Primo Foods (Tierrasanta). This was a neighborhood grocery store that had excellent ceviche by the pound.  
    * Mama's bakery and deli (North Park). Mediterranean and Lebanese. Good for lunch.

## Places and sights

* The San Diego Zoo (Balboa Park).  
* The San Diego Safari Park (Escondido). Long time locals will still call it the “Wild Animal Park”. 1800 acre wildlife sanctuary.  
* Balboa Park. The city’s large central park. Lots of stuff to do here. Parking is free throughout.  
* The Natural History Museum (Balboa Park)  
* The Air and Space Museum (Balboa Park)  
* The Science Museum (“The Fleet”)  
* USS Midway (Seaport)  
* The Maritime Museum (Seaport)  
* Liberty Station (Point Loma). Converted navy training center (used as a set for Top Gun). Lots of shops and restaurants in the Public Market.  
* The Water Conservation Garden (El Cajon)  
* San Diego Botanic Garden (Encinitas)  
* Cabrillo National Monument (Point Loma)  
* Mission Bay  
* Beaches  
    * La Jolla shores. One of the nicest beaches in town.  
    * La Jolla children's beach. To watch the seals. Don’t touch the seals though, they bite.  
    * Silver Strand (Coronado). State beach with paid parking.  
    * Torrey Pines. The hike up the hill has a great view. If you walk far enough north on the beach, you’ll get to Black’s beach, where people can be nude. If you go to Black’s beach know when the tide comes in. At high tide you’ll be cut off from Torrey Pines.
