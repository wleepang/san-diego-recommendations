# San Diego Recommendations

We lived in San Diego, CA for 11yrs. This is a list of places to eat, see, etc. Caveat: we moved away from San Diego in 2021, but return somewhat regularly (every other year or so). Things may have changed.

## Food

* Mexican  
    * [Carmen’s Mexican Food](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Carmen%27s+Mexican+Food+Clairemont+Mesa+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Clairemont Mesa). This is one of our regular taco shops. In the winter the tortilla soup is excellent.  
    * [Harvest Mexican](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Harvest+Mexican+Food+Kearny+Mesa+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Kearny Mesa). This is one of our regular taco shops. Shrimp burrito is good.  
    * [Caliente](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caliente+Mexican+Food+Sorrento+Valley+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Sorrento Valley). One of Lee’s prior regular taco shops because it was close to offices I used to work at. Adobada or carnitas burritos.  
    * [Vallarta](https://vallartaexpress.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (drive thru’s in Clairemont Mesa and Mira Mesa). Good if you need a “quick” drive through burrito. The salsa is habanero based and has a good kick.  
    * [Don Carlos](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Don+Carlos+Taco+Shop+737+Pearl+St+La+Jolla){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (La Jolla). If you’re in La Jolla and need a burrito this is a good stop. This is our regular source for California burritos. Carne asada is good too, as are the tamales.  
* Pizza  
    * [Regents Pizza](https://www.regentspizza.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (University City). \~30 taps of local craft beers. Lee used to go here after work. Had worked up to level 3 of 4 on the hot wing heat.  
    * [Bronx Pizza](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bronx+Pizza+Hillcrest+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}. The pizza only comes in one size \- large. The white pizza is amazing.  
    * [Blind Lady Ale House](https://blindladyalehouse.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Normal Heights)  
    * [Pizza Port](https://www.pizzaport.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Solana Beach and Ocean Beach). Solana Beach is the original one, parking is difficult though. Beer buddies, Swami’s IPA, and a pie.  
* Pubs and Breweries  
    * [O’Brien’s](https://obrienspub.net/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Convoy / Kearny Mesa). Irish pub with a large and rotating list of local craft beers. Occasionally they get Pliny the Younger on tap. This is a limited batch IIIPA from Russian River Brewing. Garlic fries and a burger. Skip the parking lot and find street parking or the paid lot around the corner.  
    * [Societe](https://www.societebrewing.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Kearny Mesa)  
    * [Alesmith](https://alesmith.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Miramar)  
    * [Ballast Point](https://ballastpoint.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Little Italy, Miramar)  
    * [Green Flash](https://greenflashbrew.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Sorrento Valley)  
    * [Viewpoint](https://viewpointbrewing.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Del Mar)  
    * [Duck Foot](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Duck+Foot+Brewing+Company+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}  
    * [Second Chance](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Second+Chance+Beer+Company+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}  
    * [New English](https://www.newenglishbrewing.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}  
    * [Modern times](https://www.moderntimesbeer.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (North Park)  
    * [White Labs](https://www.whitelabs.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Miramar). Go here to taste the same beer made with different yeast  
* Asian  
    * [Dumpling Inn](https://www.dumplinginn.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Convoy / Kearny Mesa). The best dumplings / dim sum in town.  
    * [Sushi Deli](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sushi+Deli+Kearny+Mesa+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} 1, 3\. (Downtown, Kearny Mesa, respectively). One of our regular sushi places.  
    * [Poke 1-½](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Poki+One+N+Half+Kearny+Mesa+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}. This is a chain (our regular location was in Kearny Mesa), but the poke is excellent for the price.  
* Other  
    * [Brazil by the Bay](https://brazilbythebay.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Sports Arena). Hole in the wall place for Brazillian food. Can’t go wrong with feijoada and a guarana with brigadeiro for desert.  
    * [El Pescador](https://www.elpescadorfishmarket.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (La Jolla). Fishmonger with fresh made sandwiches via counter service.  
    * [Caroline’s Seaside Cafe](https://carolinesseasidecafe.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (UCSD/Scripps). Good place for breakfast or lunch. Patio is right above the beach by the Scripps Pier. Parking in the nearby lot is only free on weekends, otherwise, you need to find spots on the street.  
    * [Mochinut](https://www.mochinut.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Kearny Mesa). Mochi based donuts.  
    * [Primo Foods](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Primo+Foods+Tierrasanta+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Tierrasanta). This was a neighborhood grocery store that had excellent ceviche by the pound.  
    * [Mama's bakery and deli](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mama%27s+Bakery+and+Deli+North+Park+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (North Park). Mediterranean and Lebanese. Good for lunch.

## Places and sights

* The [San Diego Zoo](https://sandiegozoo.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Balboa Park).  
* The [San Diego Safari Park](https://sdzsafaripark.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Escondido). Long time locals will still call it the “Wild Animal Park”. 1800 acre wildlife sanctuary.  
* [Balboa Park](https://balboapark.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}. The city’s large central park. Lots of stuff to do here. Parking is free throughout.  
* The [Natural History Museum](https://www.sdnhm.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Balboa Park)  
* The [Air and Space Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=San+Diego+Air+%26+Space+Museum+Balboa+Park){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Balboa Park)  
* The [Science Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fleet+Science+Center+Balboa+Park){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (“The Fleet”)  
* [USS Midway](https://www.midway.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Seaport)  
* The [Maritime Museum](https://sdmaritime.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Seaport)  
* [Liberty Station](https://libertystation.com/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Point Loma). Converted navy training center (used as a set for Top Gun). Lots of shops and restaurants in the Public Market.  
* The [Water Conservation Garden](https://thegarden.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (El Cajon)  
* [San Diego Botanic Garden](https://sdbg.org/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Encinitas)  
* [Cabrillo National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/cabr/){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Point Loma)  
* [Mission Bay](https://www.sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/parks/regional/missionbay){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}  
* Beaches  
    * [La Jolla shores](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=La+Jolla+Shores+Beach+San+Diego){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}. One of the nicest beaches in town.  
    * [La Jolla children's beach](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Children%27s+Pool+Beach+La+Jolla){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}. To watch the seals. Don’t touch the seals though, they bite.  
    * [Silver Strand](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Silver+Strand+State+Beach+Coronado){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"} (Coronado). State beach with paid parking.  
    * [Torrey Pines](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Torrey+Pines+State+Natural+Reserve+La+Jolla){target=_blank rel="noopener noreferrer"}. The hike up the hill has a great view. If you walk far enough north on the beach, you’ll get to Black’s beach, where people can be nude. If you go to Black’s beach know when the tide comes in. At high tide you’ll be cut off from Torrey Pines.
